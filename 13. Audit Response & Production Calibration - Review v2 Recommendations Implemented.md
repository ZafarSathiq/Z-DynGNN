## 2. Recommendation 1: Terminology Disambiguation

The codebase and output certificates now enforce strict separation of terminology:

* **`Topology-Conditioned Conformal Prediction`**:
  * **Role:** Biophysical diagnostic and structural stratification.
  * **Function:** Evaluates uncertainty bounds broken down by local coordination degree ($d_i$) and Asymmetric Dynamic Coupling ($\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}$), enabling researchers to inspect model confidence across solvent-exposed loops, intermediate packing, dense core, and allosteric driver/sensor motifs.
* **`Cluster-Adjusted Conformal Prediction`**:
  * **Role:** The formal mathematical guarantee engine.
  * **Function:** Accounts for residue-level cluster sampling under position-disjoint partitioning by aggregating within-position non-conformity scores ($q_{\text{agg}} = 0.90$). Guarantees marginal finite-sample coverage $\mathbb{P}(y_i \in \mathcal{C}(x_i)) \ge 1 - \alpha$ on completely unseen positions.
* **Output Artifact Implementation:**
  `results/conformal_certificates_{condition}.csv` outputs both columns side-by-side:
  `bound_cluster_adjusted` (for the certified guarantee) and `bound_topology_conditioned` (for structural analysis).

## 4. Recommendation 3: Hybrid Conformal Interval Scaling

As recommended in Review v2, Z-DynGNN preserves a **hybrid formulation**:
$$B_i = \hat{q}_{\text{cluster}} \cdot \hat{\sigma}(x_i)$$
$$\mathcal{C}(x_i) = \left[\hat{\mu}(x_i) - B_i, \; \hat{\mu}(x_i) + B_i\right]$$

### Why the Hybrid Formulation Works:
1. **The Global Cluster Quantile ($\hat{q}_{\text{cluster}}$):** Calibrated over the 32 position clusters at $q_{\text{agg}} = 0.90$, providing the rigorous exchangeability bridge across held-out positions.
2. **The Local Aleatoric Scale ($\hat{\sigma}(x_i)$):** Predicted by Z-DynGNN's heteroscedastic scale head based on the residue's 3D contact graph, continuous RBF distances, and $\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}$ directional coupling.
3. **Synergy:** Residues in rigid structural environments receive narrower intervals, while flexible allosteric loops receive safety-expanded intervals, without violating the marginal coverage theorem.

## 5. Recommendation 4: Clinical Priorities for Step 7

The reviewer emphasized:
> *"The most important remaining check is still the clinical one: how often do the certified intervals correctly contain (or correctly exclude) known BLDB resistance phenotypes and the M154L allosteric variant relative to MIC breakpoints. Interval width relative to the noise ceiling is secondary to that utility question."*

In accordance with this directive, **Step 7** prioritizes three concrete clinical benchmarks:
1. **BLDB Clinical Variants Benchmark:** Evaluate certified intervals on the 69 clinical NDM variants (NDM-1 through NDM-69) cataloged in the Beta-Lactamase Database.
2. **Allosteric Resistant Variant (M154L):** Specifically assess whether certified intervals at position 154 correctly capture the enhanced carbapenemase phenotype while identifying allosteric dynamics coupling to active site zinc loops.
3. **MIC Resistance Classification:** Map certified fitness intervals $[\hat{\mu} - B_i, \hat{\mu} + B_i]$ onto clinical resistance breakpoints (Resistant vs. Susceptible) to compute true positive and false negative decision rates under certified confidence.
