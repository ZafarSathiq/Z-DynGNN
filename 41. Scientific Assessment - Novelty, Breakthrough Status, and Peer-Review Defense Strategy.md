## 5. What Prevents Overclaiming as a "Biological Nobel Miracle"

To maintain absolute credibility in peer review, the manuscript must be honest about what the system does *not* do:
1. **It is an algorithmic diagnostic shield, not a new drug:** It does not discover a newly synthesized chemical molecule that cures NDM-1; it certifies which mutations resist existing drugs.
2. **M154L point prediction remains biased:** Point predictions on M154L underestimate fitness (predicting $-5.86$ vs. true $+0.49$). Containment succeeds because the certified confidence bound is wide enough to absorb the error.
3. **M248L is missed on Meropenem:** M248L ($+1.22$) exceeded the upper bound ($+1.14$). This is mathematically compliant with the 95% nominal confidence rate (21/22 = 95.5%), but it remains a known outlier.
