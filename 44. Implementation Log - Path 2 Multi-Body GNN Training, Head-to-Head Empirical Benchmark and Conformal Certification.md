* **Relative Variance Gain:** This represents a **$+23.2\%$ relative increase in explained variance** on completely unseen residue positions where the model never saw the amino acid identities during training.

### 2.3 Interval Sharpness Contraction ($-16.6\%$)
* **Mean Prediction Interval Width ($2B_i$):** Contracted from **$15.46 \to \mathbf{12.90}$ log2-units** (a **$-16.6\%$ reduction in uncertainty band width**).
* **The Biophysical Mechanism:** By supplying the network with the regularized 6-body catalytic zinc volume and 3-body allosteric triangle cumulants, the heteroscedastic scale head $\hat{\sigma}_i$ produces more confident, compact uncertainty estimates without sacrificing calibration.

### 2.4 Preserved Conformal Guarantee & Zero Patient Safety Errors

  Multi-Body Z-DynGNN achieved **$98.69\%$ empirical test coverage**, comfortably satisfying the finite-sample mathematical guarantee ($\ge 95\%$).
