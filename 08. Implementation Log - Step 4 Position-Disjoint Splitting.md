## 1. Executive Summary

Step 4 implements the strictly position-disjoint partitioning pipeline for NDM-1 mutation fitness data.

In protein deep mutational scanning, naive random splits across individual mutations cause severe **positional data leakage**: models memorize site-average conservation instead of learning epistasis, and conformal exchangeability between calibration and test sets is compromised.

Step 4 partitions the **228 residue positions** rather than individual mutations, guaranteeing:
$$\mathcal{P}_{\text{train}} \cap \mathcal{P}_{\text{calib}} = \emptyset, \quad \mathcal{P}_{\text{train}} \cap \mathcal{P}_{\text{test}} = \emptyset, \quad \mathcal{P}_{\text{calib}} \cap \mathcal{P}_{\text{test}} = \emptyset$$

All mathematical invariants were verified across the 228 resolved positions (residues 43–270) and 4,271 missense variants:
* **Canonical Stratified 70/15/15 Split:** 163 train positions (3,062 mutations, 71.7%), 32 calibration positions (597 mutations, 14.0%), and 33 test positions (612 mutations, 14.3%).
* **Balanced Stratification:** Positions were stratified across the 11 structural/dynamics strata $\mathcal{S}(i)$ computed in Step 3, ensuring balanced packing and driver/sensor representation across all partitions.
* **5-Fold Cross-Validation Architecture:** Built and cached 5-fold position-disjoint splits covering 100% of all 228 positions exactly once in held-out test partitions.
* **Test Verification:** 14/14 unit and integration tests passing in 12.45s.
