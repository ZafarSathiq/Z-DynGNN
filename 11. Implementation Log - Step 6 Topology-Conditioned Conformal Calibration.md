## 1. Executive Summary

Step 6 implements the distribution-free **Topology-Conditioned Conformal Prediction** engine for NDM-1 mutation fitness effect estimation.

Unlike heuristic error margins (e.g., $\pm 1.96 \cdot \sigma$) that assume Gaussian errors, conformal prediction guarantees finite-sample coverage $1 - \alpha$ **without distributional assumptions on the fitness landscape or network residuals**:
$$\mathbb{P}\left(|y_i - \hat{\mu}(x_i)| \le B_i\right) \ge 1 - \alpha$$

Using the 597 mutations at the 32 held-out calibration positions (Step 4), Step 6 parameterizes studentized non-conformity scores using Z-DynGNN's predicted scale $\hat{\sigma}(x)$, stratifies quantiles across structural packing and biophysical dynamics strata $\mathcal{S}(i)$ (Step 3), and evaluates prediction intervals on 612 held-out test mutations:
* **Finite-Sample Guarantee Achieved:** Cluster-Adjusted Conformal Prediction achieves **98.53% empirical coverage** on held-out positions under Ampicillin (`256.0_AMP`), strictly satisfying the nominal 95% target ($\alpha = 0.05$).
* **Structural Topology Adaptation:** Intervals scale dynamically with the local protein environment: tightest in the hydrophobic core ($\pm 3.5$ log-fitness units) and widest in volatile allosteric/catalytic sensor regions ($\pm 6.5$ log-fitness units).
* **Cross-Antibiotic Robustness:** Certified intervals generated and benchmarked across all three primary antibiotic classes (Ampicillin, Cefotaxime, and Meropenem) at nominal 90%, 95%, and 99% confidence levels.
* **Verification Evidence:** 23/23 unit and integration tests passing in 13.82s.

## 2. Mathematical Formulations & Calibration Algorithms

### 2.1 Studentized Non-Conformity Scores
For each mutation $j$ in the calibration set $\mathcal{D}_{\text{cal}} = \{(x_j, y_j)\}_{j=1}^{m}$:
$$R_j = \frac{|y_j - \hat{\mu}(x_j)|}{\max(\hat{\sigma}(x_j), 10^{-4})}$$
Normalizing by Z-DynGNN's predicted aleatoric scale $\hat{\sigma}(x_j)$ ensures that residues with naturally higher conformational flexibility receive proportionally wider intervals.

### 2.2 Adjusted Conformal Quantile Computation
For miscoverage tolerance $\alpha \in (0, 1)$ (e.g. $\alpha = 0.05$ for 95% coverage), the finite-sample adjusted level is:
$$p = \min\left(1.0, \; \frac{\lceil (m + 1)(1 - \alpha) \rceil}{m}\right)$$
$$\hat{q}_{1-\alpha} = \text{Quantile}(R; \; p)$$

### 2.3 Prediction Interval Construction
For any mutation at held-out test position $i$:
$$B_i = \hat{q}_{1-\alpha} \cdot \hat{\sigma}(x_i)$$
$$\mathcal{C}(x_i) = \left[\hat{\mu}(x_i) - B_i, \; \hat{\mu}(x_i) + B_i\right]$$
