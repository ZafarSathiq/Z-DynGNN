### 3.1 PDB Coordinate & Contact Graph Parser (`src/zdyngnn/data/pdb_parser.py`)
* **Source:** `DMS Data NDM-1/Strutural data/3SPU.pdb`
* **Target:** Chain A, Residues 43–270 (228 resolved residues; residues 1–42 correspond to the N-terminal signal peptide absent in the crystal structure; residues 271–274 represent C-terminal purification tags).
* **Outputs in `PDBStructureData`:**
  * `coords`: Array of shape `(228, 3)` ($C_\alpha$ positions in Ångströms).
  * `dist_matrix`: Pairwise Euclidean distance matrix of shape `(228, 228)` ($D_{ij} = \|\mathbf{r}_i - \mathbf{r}_j\|$).
  * `adj_matrix`: Symmetric binary contact adjacency matrix with cutoff $r_c = 8.0$ Å ($A_{ij} = \mathbb{I}(D_{ij} \le 8.0 \land i \neq j)$).
  * `degrees`: Coordination number vector ($d_i = \sum_{j \neq i} A_{ij}$).
  * `b_factors`: Isotropic temperature factor vector of shape `(228,)`.
  * `sequence`: 228-character 1-letter amino acid sequence.

### 3.3 Empirical Noise Ceiling Quantification (`src/zdyngnn/data/noise_ceiling.py`)
Experimental assays contain measurement error ($\epsilon_i$). To prevent artificial claims of performance beyond physical reproducibility, the noise ceiling establishes the theoretical maximum signal variance:
$$\text{Var}(y) = \text{Var}(f) + E[\sigma_\epsilon^2]$$
$$R^2_{\max} = 1 - \frac{E[\sigma_\epsilon^2]}{\text{Var}(y)}, \quad r_{\max} = \sqrt{R^2_{\max}}, \quad \text{SNR} = \frac{\text{Var}(f)}{E[\sigma_\epsilon^2]}$$

**Empirical Results Across the 9 Drug Conditions ($N = 4,248$ complete replicate pairs):**

| Drug Condition | Total Variance $\text{Var}(y)$ | Mean Error Variance $E[\sigma_\epsilon^2]$ | Signal Variance $\text{Var}(f)$ | $R^2_{\max}$ | $r_{\max}$ | SNR |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **32.0_AMP** | 4.909 | 0.584 | 4.325 | **0.881** | **0.939** | 7.40 |
| **256.0_AMP** | 9.753 | 0.264 | 9.489 | **0.973** | **0.986** | 35.97 |
| **512.0_AMP** | 10.385 | 1.643 | 8.743 | **0.842** | **0.918** | 5.32 |
| **2.0_CTX** | 4.178 | 0.145 | 4.033 | **0.965** | **0.983** | 27.84 |
| **16.0_CTX** | 6.890 | 0.218 | 6.672 | **0.968** | **0.984** | 30.66 |
| **32.0_CTX** | 9.385 | 0.524 | 8.861 | **0.944** | **0.972** | 16.92 |
| **0.0625_MEM** | 3.872 | 0.211 | 3.662 | **0.946** | **0.972** | 17.37 |
| **0.25_MEM** | 6.619 | 0.444 | 6.175 | **0.933** | **0.966** | 13.91 |
| **0.5_MEM** | 8.493 | 1.207 | 7.286 | **0.858** | **0.926** | 6.03 |

**Key Finding:** Primary benchmark conditions (`256.0_AMP`, `16.0_CTX`, `0.25_MEM`) demonstrate high signal-to-noise ratios ($\text{SNR} \ge 13.9\text{--}36.0$) and $R^2_{\max} \ge 0.933\text{--}0.973$.
