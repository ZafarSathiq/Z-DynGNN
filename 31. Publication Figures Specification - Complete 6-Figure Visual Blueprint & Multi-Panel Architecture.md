* **Panel 3A (Calibration Non-Conformity Distribution):**
  * *Visual Form:* Histogram and kernel density curve of studentized calibration scores $R_j = \frac{|y_j - \hat{\mu}_j|}{\max(\hat{\sigma}_j, \varepsilon)}$ across the 32 calibration positions, highlighting the finite-sample cutoff quantile.
