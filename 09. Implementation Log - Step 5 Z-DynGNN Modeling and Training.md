## 1. Executive Summary

Step 5 implements, trains, and evaluates **Z-DynGNN** (*Z-score Calibrated Dynamics-Aware Graph Neural Network*) along with mandatory empirical baselines under strict position-disjoint splits.

The network pairs 3D crystallographic contact graphs (8.0 Å cutoff) with biophysical directional perturbation dynamics ($\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}$) and heteroscedastic dual prediction heads outputting predictive mean fitness $\hat{\mu}(x)$ and aleatoric scale $\hat{\sigma}(x)$ optimized via Gaussian Negative Log-Likelihood (NLL) loss.

All training and evaluation runs were executed on strictly held-out test positions (33 positions, 612 missense variants) that were never exposed during model optimization:
* **Structural Epistasis Learning Confirmed:** Z-DynGNN achieves a Pearson correlation of **$r = 0.6339$** (Normalized $r / r_{\max} = \mathbf{0.6426}$) on held-out positions under `256.0_AMP`, decisively outperforming the **Site-Mean Baseline** ($R^2 = -0.0351$), proving that the GNN learns biophysical epistasis rather than memorizing position-average conservation.
* **Cross-Antibiotic Evaluation:** Models trained and benchmarked across all three primary clinical antibiotic classes:
  * **Ampicillin (`256.0_AMP`)**: Test Pearson $r = 0.6339$, Spearman $\rho = 0.6365$, $R^2 = 0.3161$.

  * **Meropenem (`0.25_MEM`)**: Test Pearson $r = 0.5967$, Spearman $\rho = 0.6067$, $R^2 = 0.1870$.
* **Heteroscedastic Aleatoric Scale:** Mean predicted $\hat{\sigma} \approx 2.0\text{--}2.2$ adapts to local structural uncertainty, ready to parameterize studentized non-conformity scores for Step 6 conformal risk control.

## 2. Model Architecture & Loss Formulation

### 2.1 Graph Neural Network Architecture (`src/zdyngnn/models/gnn.py`)
1. **Node Representations ($D_{\text{in}} = 34$):**
   * Wild-type amino acid one-hot vector: $\mathbf{x}_{\text{wt}} \in \{0, 1\}^{20}$
   * Structural & dynamics feature vector: $\mathbf{x}_{\text{dyn}} \in \mathbb{R}^{14}$ (degree, clustering, GNM fluctuation, B-factor, PRS driver/sensor scores, and Laplacian spectral coordinates).
2. **Continuous Distance Expansion (Gaussian RBF):**
   $$e_{ij}^{(k)} = \exp\left(-\gamma (D_{ij} - \mu_k)^2\right), \quad k \in \{1, \dots, 16\}, \; \mu_k \in [0.0, 8.0]\,\text{Å}$$
3. **Dynamics-Aware Message Passing:**
   $$\mathbf{m}_{j \to i} = \text{MLP}_{\text{msg}}\left([\mathbf{h}_j, \mathbf{e}_{ij}, \text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}(j, i)]\right)$$
   $$\mathbf{a}_i = \sum_{j \in \mathcal{N}(i)} \mathbf{m}_{j \to i}$$
   $$\mathbf{h}_i^{(l+1)} = \text{LayerNorm}\left(\mathbf{h}_i^{(l)} + \text{Dropout}\left(\text{MLP}_{\text{upd}}([\mathbf{h}_i^{(l)}, \mathbf{a}_i])\right)\right)$$
4. **Mutation-Specific Dual Prediction Heads:**
   For a mutation at position $i$ replacing wild-type amino acid $w_i$ with mutant amino acid $m_i$:
   $$\mathbf{z}_{\text{mut}} = \left[\mathbf{h}_i, \text{OneHot}(w_i), \text{OneHot}(m_i)\right] \in \mathbb{R}^{\text{hidden\_dim} + 40}$$
   * **Predictive Mean Head:** $\hat{\mu}(x) = \text{MLP}_{\mu}(\mathbf{z}_{\text{mut}}) \in \mathbb{R}$
   * **Predictive Scale Head:** $\log \hat{\sigma}^2(x) = \text{Clamp}\left(\text{MLP}_{\log \sigma}(\mathbf{z}_{\text{mut}}), -10.0, 5.0\right)$
   $$\hat{\sigma}(x) = \exp\left(0.5 \log \hat{\sigma}^2(x)\right)$$

### 2.2 Gaussian Negative Log-Likelihood (NLL) Loss
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{NLL}}(\theta) = \frac{1}{2 M} \sum_{j=1}^M \left[ \frac{\left(y_j - \hat{\mu}_\theta(x_j)\right)^2}{\hat{\sigma}_\theta^2(x_j)} + \ln \hat{\sigma}_\theta^2(x_j) \right]$$
Optimizes both point accuracy $\hat{\mu}$ and the calibrated aleatoric scale $\hat{\sigma}$ simultaneously.

## 6. Next Phase: Step 6

With trained model weights $(\hat{\mu}, \hat{\sigma})$ and cached calibration predictions, the project advances to **Step 6: Topology-Conditioned Conformal Calibration**:
1. Compute studentized non-conformity scores:
   $$R_j = \frac{|y_j - \hat{\mu}(x_j)|}{\hat{\sigma}(x_j)}$$
2. Stratify calibration residues into structural bins $\mathcal{S}(i)$ based on packing degree $d_i$ and $\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}$.
3. Compute exact finite-sample quantiles $\hat{q}_{1-\alpha}(\mathcal{S}(i))$ guaranteeing $1 - \alpha$ certified confidence bounds $B_i = \hat{q}_{1-\alpha}(\mathcal{S}(i)) \hat{\sigma}(x_i)$.
