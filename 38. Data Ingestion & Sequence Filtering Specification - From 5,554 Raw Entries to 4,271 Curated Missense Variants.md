3. **770 rows** represent **missense mutations in the cleaved N-terminal signal peptide** (residues 1 to 42), which are physically cleaved during periplasmic translocation and absent from all X-ray crystal structures.
4. **4,271 rows** constitute the **final curated missense substitutions across the mature catalytic domain** (residues 43 to 270), representing a **98.59% saturation completeness** of the folded enzyme.

## 3. Biological & Crystallographic Rationale

### 3.1 Why Synonymous Controls (244 rows) Were Removed
In saturation mutagenesis libraries, wild-type sequences are deliberately synthesized and sequenced alongside variants to establish the baseline wild-type fitness ($y = 0.0$).
* In the raw Excel file, these rows are labeled with `identity = 'syn'` (e.g., `M1M`, `A2A`, `K3K`).
* In machine learning for variant effect prediction, the task is:
  $$\text{Predict fitness change } \Delta y \text{ given substitution } a_{\text{wt}} \to a_{\text{mut}} \ (a_{\text{wt}} \ne a_{\text{mut}})$$
* A wild-type control where no amino acid changes is by definition not a substitution. Retaining them would artificially inflate prediction metrics by testing the network on trivial identity mappings ($a_{\text{wt}} \to a_{\text{wt}} \implies y = 0.0$).

### 3.2 Why Nonsense Stop Codons (268 rows) Were Removed
Codon substitutions that introduce stop codons (`TAA`, `TAG`, `TGA`) produce premature termination of translation.
* In the raw dataset, these are marked with `mut = '*'`.
* A stop codon causes premature truncation of the polypeptide chain, resulting in truncated fragments rather than a folded enzyme carrying a substituted side chain.
* Structural Graph Neural Networks encode substitutions via a 20-dimensional canonical amino acid alphabet. Nonsense codons cannot be represented in a 20-amino-acid transition tensor and represent complete loss of synthesis rather than missense chemistry.

### 3.3 Why Positions 1 to 42 (770 rows) Were Filtered
NDM-1 is synthesized as a precursor pre-protein of 270 amino acids:
* **Residues 1 to 28:** Hydrophobic N-terminal signal peptide.
* **Residues 29 to 42:** Flexible membrane-tethering linker and lipobox motif (`Cys26/Cys42`).
* **Residues 43 to 270:** The catalytic globular metallo-$\beta$-lactamase domain.

#### The Biochemical & Structural Reality:
1. **Periplasmic Translocation & Cleavage:** During bacterial maturation in *Klebsiella pneumoniae* or *E. coli*, the 42-residue N-terminal anchor is cleaved by signal peptidase II to release the functional globular enzyme into the periplasm.
2. **Absence in X-ray Crystallography:** Because residues 1 to 42 are cleaved or highly disordered, **they are physically absent from the high-resolution crystal structure (PDB 3SPU)**.
3. **Mathematical Requirement of GNNs:** A 3D Graph Neural Network requires Cartesian coordinates $\mathbf{r}_i = (x_i, y_i, z_i) \in \mathbb{R}^3$ to compute:
   * 8.0 Å Euclidean contact edges $A_{ij}$
   * Continuous Gaussian RBF distance encodings $\phi(d_{ij})$
   * Elastic Network Model Kirchhoff and Hessian matrices ($\Gamma$ and $H$)
   * Perturbation Response Scanning displacement matrices ($S_{ij}$)
4. Because no physical atomic coordinates exist for positions 1 through 42, placing them into a 3D structural graph is mathematically impossible. Filtering these 770 unmodeled positions leaves **exactly 4,271 missense substitutions**.

## 4. Saturation Mutagenesis Completeness Analysis

To evaluate how thoroughly the 4,271 curated variants cover the physical structure of NDM-1:
* Number of resolved residue positions in PDB 3SPU Chain A: **$N = 228$ positions** (residues 43 through 270).
* For each residue, there are 19 possible non-wild-type amino acid substitutions:
  $$\text{Theoretical Maximum Missense Mutations} = 228 \times 19 = \mathbf{4,332\text{ mutations}}$$
* Number of missense mutations successfully measured in Chen et al. 2024: **4,271 mutations**.
* **Saturation Completeness:**
  $$\text{Completeness Rate} = \frac{4,271}{4,332} = \mathbf{98.59\%}$$

Only **61 possible substitutions (1.41%)** across the entire 228-residue mature enzyme were unobserved due to low sequencing read depth or library synthesis dropout. The curated dataset covers **98.6% of all mathematically possible single amino acid mutations in NDM-1**.
