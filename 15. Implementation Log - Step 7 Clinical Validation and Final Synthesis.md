### 3.2 Directional Allosteric Dynamic Coupling ($\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}$) to Catalytic Zinc Ions
Perturbation Response Scanning on the ANM Hessian confirms directional communication from position 154 to all six active-site catalytic zinc coordinating residues:
$$\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}(\text{active\_site}, 154) = \text{DCI}(\text{active\_site}, 154) - \text{DCI}(154, \text{active\_site})$$

1. **$\text{His120}$ (Zinc Ligand 1):** $\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}} = -0.4007 \rightarrow$ **Receptive Dynamic Sensor**
2. **$\text{His122}$ (Zinc Ligand 2):** $\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}} = -0.1723 \rightarrow$ **Receptive Dynamic Sensor**
3. **$\text{Asp124}$ (Zinc Ligand 3):** $\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}} = -0.2747 \rightarrow$ **Receptive Dynamic Sensor**
4. **$\text{His189}$ (Zinc Ligand 4):** $\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}} = -0.3526 \rightarrow$ **Receptive Dynamic Sensor**
5. **$\text{Cys208}$ (Zinc Ligand 5):** $\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}} = -0.5185 \rightarrow$ **Receptive Dynamic Sensor**
6. **$\text{His250}$ (Zinc Ligand 6):** $\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}} = -0.4021 \rightarrow$ **Receptive Dynamic Sensor**

* **Biophysical Significance:** Negative coupling values confirm that position 154 functions as an **allosteric driver**, while the catalytic zinc pocket acts as a **receptive dynamic sensor**, explaining why M154L stabilizes the zinc pocket without steric clash in the catalytic site.
