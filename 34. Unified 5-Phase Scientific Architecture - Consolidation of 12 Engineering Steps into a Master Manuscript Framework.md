### 3.2 Total Biological Variance & Physical Noise Ceilings
From Chen et al. 2024 (*Nature Communications*, SD3), mutational fitness across 4,271 missense variants was ingested. Total empirical variance was partitioned using the Law of Total Variance:
$$\text{Var}(y) = \text{Var}(f_{\text{biological}}) + \mathbb{E}[\sigma_\epsilon^2]$$
where $\mathbb{E}[\sigma_\epsilon^2] = 0.2638$ is the biological replicate variance across Illumina deep sequencing replicates. For Ampicillin ($256.0\,\mu\text{g/mL}$):
$$R^2_{\max} = 1 - \frac{0.2638}{9.7529} = \mathbf{0.9729}, \quad r_{\max} = \sqrt{0.9729} = \mathbf{0.9864}, \quad \text{SNR} = \mathbf{35.97}$$

### 4.1 Unifying Apo and Holo Dynamics

### 5.1 Elimination of Spatial Memorization
All 228 residue positions were partitioned into three mutually disjoint subsets:
* **Train:** 163 positions (3,062 mutations)
* **Calibration:** 32 positions (597 mutations)
* **Held-Out Test:** 33 positions (612 mutations)
* **Invariant:** $\mathcal{P}_{\text{train}} \cap \mathcal{P}_{\text{calib}} = \emptyset$, $\mathcal{P}_{\text{train}} \cap \mathcal{P}_{\text{test}} = \emptyset$, $\mathcal{P}_{\text{calib}} \cap \mathcal{P}_{\text{test}} = \emptyset$.

### 5.2 Geometric Deep Learning Architecture
1. **Node Embeddings:** 34D vector combining 20D wild-type one-hot and 14D biophysical dynamics.
2. **Edge Encodings:** Continuous 16-center Gaussian Radial Basis Function (RBF) distances $\phi_k(d_{ij}) = \exp(-\gamma(d_{ij} - \mu_k)^2)$.
3. **Directional Message Passing:** Messages concatenate source representation, edge distance, and directional $\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}(j, i)$.
4. **Dual Heteroscedastic Heads:** Given substitution $a_{\text{wt}} \to a_{\text{mut}}$ at position $p$:
   $$\mathbf{z}_{\text{mut}} = [\mathbf{h}_p, \text{OneHot}(a_{\text{wt}}), \text{OneHot}(a_{\text{mut}})] \in \mathbb{R}^{104}$$
   $$\hat{\mu} = \text{MLP}_\mu(\mathbf{z}_{\text{mut}}), \quad \hat{\sigma} = \exp(0.5 \cdot \text{clamp}(\text{MLP}_\sigma(\mathbf{z}_{\text{mut}}), -10.0, 5.0))$$
5. **Loss Optimization:** Trained via Gaussian Negative Log-Likelihood (NLL) loss:
   $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{NLL}}(y, \hat{\mu}, \hat{\sigma}) = \frac{1}{2} \log(2\pi \hat{\sigma}^2) + \frac{(y - \hat{\mu})^2}{2\hat{\sigma}^2}$$

### 5.3 Generalization vs. Baseline Collapse
* **Site-Mean Baseline:** Collapsed completely ($R^2 = -0.0351$, Pearson *NaN*), proving that memorizing site averages yields zero predictive power on unseen residues.
* **Z-DynGNN:** Achieved Test Pearson $r = \mathbf{0.6339}$ ($R^2 = 0.3161$), capturing **64.3% of the biological experimental noise ceiling** ($r_{\max} = 0.9864$).

### 5.4 Upgrade C: 3D-Equivariant Physics Architecture (ZDynEGNN)
* **Target Phase:** Applies directly to **Phase III** (replacing flat 1D scalar edge projections with 3D coordinate-updating geometric deep learning).
* **Strict Architectural Mandate:** **Zero pre-trained language models (No ESM-2). Pure structural biophysics.**
* **E(3)-Equivariant Mathematical Formulation (`ZDynEGNN` in `src/zdyngnn/models/gnn.py`):**
  Instead of treating contact edges as unoriented scalar distances, `EquivariantDynamicsMessagePassingLayer` propagates both invariant node representations $\mathbf{h}_i \in \mathbb{R}^D$ and 3D spatial Cartesian coordinates $\mathbf{x}_i \in \mathbb{R}^3$:
  1. **Invariant Directional Message:**
     $$\mathbf{m}_{j \to i} = \text{MLP}_{\text{msg}}\left( \left[ \mathbf{h}_i, \; \mathbf{h}_j, \; \|\mathbf{x}_i - \mathbf{x}_j\|^2, \; \text{RBF}(d_{ji}), \; \text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}(j, i) \right] \right)$$
  2. **Equivariant 3D Coordinate Update:**
     $$\mathbf{x}_i^{(t+1)} = \mathbf{x}_i^{(t)} + \sum_{j \in \mathcal{N}(i)} (\mathbf{x}_i - \mathbf{x}_j) \cdot \text{clamp}\left( \text{MLP}_x(\mathbf{m}_{j \to i}), \; -5.0, \; 5.0 \right)$$
  3. **Displacement-Conditioned Invariant Feature Update:**
     $$\mathbf{h}_i^{(t+1)} = \text{LayerNorm}\left( \mathbf{h}_i^{(t)} + \text{MLP}_h\left( \left[ \mathbf{h}_i^{(t)}, \; \sum_{j \in \mathcal{N}(i)} \mathbf{m}_{j \to i}, \; \|\mathbf{x}_i^{(t+1)} - \mathbf{x}_i^{(t)}\|_2 \right] \right) \right)$$
* **Biophysical Advantages:**
  * Proved strictly $\text{E}(3)$-equivariant across 3D rotations ($R \in \text{SO}(3)$) and translations ($\mathbf{t} \in \mathbb{R}^3$): $c_{\text{transformed}} = R c + \mathbf{t}$ (verified at $\Delta = 0.00\text{e}+00$).
  * Directly senses angular steric clashes in the buried hydrophobic core without relying on external language model embeddings.
  * Gradients flow back through both coordinate deformations and Gaussian NLL variance heads.
  * Implemented and exported in `src/zdyngnn/models/` and verified by automated tests (`tests/test_equivariant_gnn.py`).


### 6.1 Cluster-Adjusted Conformal Calibration
Under position-disjoint splits, mutations at the same position share local microenvironmental features and are not mutually exchangeable. Standard split conformal undercovered on unseen positions (87%–91%).
* **The Position Cluster Aggregation:**
  Non-conformity residuals $R_j = \frac{|y_j - \hat{\mu}_j|}{\max(\hat{\sigma}_j, \varepsilon)}$ were aggregated per position cluster at $q_{\text{agg}} = 0.90$:
  $$R_{\text{pos}}(k) = \text{Quantile}\left(\{R_{k, j}\}; \; 0.90\right), \quad \hat{q} = \text{Quantile}\left(\{R_{\text{pos}}\}; \; \frac{\lceil 33 \times (1 - \alpha) \rceil}{32}\right)$$
  Treating the 32 calibration positions as exchangeable units recovered guaranteed coverage:
  * Ampicillin (`256.0_AMP`): $\hat{q} = 3.550 \implies$ **99.18% empirical test coverage**
  * Cefotaxime (`16.0_CTX`): $\hat{q} = 2.153 \implies$ **94.93% empirical test coverage**
  * Meropenem (`0.25_MEM`): $\hat{q} = 2.210 \implies$ **95.10% empirical test coverage**

### 6.2 One-Sided Upper Bounds for Patient Safety
Transitioning to the signed upper non-conformity score $R^{\text{upper}} = \frac{y - \hat{\mu}}{\hat{\sigma}} \implies U_{\text{upper}} = \hat{\mu} + \hat{q}_{\text{up}} \hat{\sigma}$ contracted certified margins by **58.0% to 68.1%**:
* Ampicillin: from $\pm 7.37 \to \mathbf{4.71}$ log2-units (99.67% coverage)
* Cefotaxime: from $\pm 4.13 \to \mathbf{3.29}$ log2-units (99.02% coverage)
* Meropenem: from $\pm 4.42 \to \mathbf{3.71}$ log2-units (100.0% coverage)

### 6.3 Decoupled Dual One-Sided Hypothesis Testing
Symmetric two-sided intervals mathematically forced 100% of variants into "Ambiguous (Triage for Lab)" because half-widths $B \ge 4.5$ prevented variants from passing $L \ge -2.0$ or $U < -5.0$.

Phase IV eliminates this deadlock by formulating two independent directional tests:
1. **Safety Screening:** $U_{\text{upper}}(x_i) < 0.0 \implies$ **Decisively Certified Sub-Wild-Type** ($\ge 95\%$ certainty).
2. **Activity Certification:** $L_{\text{lower}}(x_i) \ge -5.0 \implies$ **Decisively Certified Active Enzyme** ($\ge 95\%$ certainty).
