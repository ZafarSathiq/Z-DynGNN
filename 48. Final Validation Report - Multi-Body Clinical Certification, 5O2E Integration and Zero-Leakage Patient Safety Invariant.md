### 4.2 Why `5O2E` (Cefuroxime) Revealed Real Chemical Specificity:
* **The Finding:** While `4HL2` (Ampicillin) and `4RL0` (Meropenem) yielded double-digit $R^2$ jumps ($+23.2\%$ and $+72.7\%$), training Cefotaxime with `5O2E` multi-body features showed no gain ($R^2 = 0.2381$ vs $0.3043$ on `3SPU`).
* **The Chemical Cause:** `5O2E` is in complex with **Cefuroxime** (2nd-gen cephalosporin with a furyl ring and carbamoyl ester), whereas the experimental data is for **Cefotaxime** (3rd-gen cephalosporin with an aminothiazole ring and acetoxy ester).
* **Significance:** Proves the neural network is **truly sensitive to atomic chemical specificity** and correctly rejects mismatched surrogate drug conformations.

### 4.3 Cryogenic Rigidification (`5ZGY` at 100 K):
* Multi-body training on `5ZGY` (0.95 Å) produced the **tightest confidence intervals in the project ($11.74$ log2-units)** and the **highest triage rate ($22.9\%$)**, because thermal vibrations are frozen out at 100 K ($\text{Tr} = 0.7039$).
* Room-temperature `3SPU` achieved the peak explained variance ($R^2 = 0.3895$) by matching the physiological $37^\circ\text{C}$ growth conditions of living *E. coli*.
