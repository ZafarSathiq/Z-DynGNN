## 3. Pharmacological Significance of Concentration-Specific Models

As proved in Section 1, drug concentrations are physical selective pressures that cannot be averaged:
1. **Low Doses ($32\,\text{AMP}, 2\,\text{CTX}, 0.0625\,\text{MEM}$):**  
   Characterized by tight phenotypic distributions around wild-type fitness with compact interval widths ($2B_i \approx 6.3\text{--}6.8$ log2-units), identifying hypersensitive mutations that destroy basal enzymatic survival.
2. **Mid Doses ($256\,\text{AMP}, 16\,\text{CTX}, 0.25\,\text{MEM}$):**  
   The primary clinical therapeutic anchors, maximizing phenotypic separation and capturing up to $67.0\%$ of the biological noise ceiling.
3. **High Doses ($512\,\text{AMP}, 32\,\text{CTX}, 0.5\,\text{MEM}$):**  
   Provides the extreme selective ceiling, isolating super-resistant outbreak drivers like `M154L` on Meropenem ($+0.32$ selective advantage) and proving that dose escalation fails against active allosteric variants.
