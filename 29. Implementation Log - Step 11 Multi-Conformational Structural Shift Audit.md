## 1. Executive Summary & Objectives Achieved

Step 11 executes the multi-conformational structural shift comparative audit of the **Z-DynGNN** framework, expanding biophysical dynamics beyond the static unliganded Apo crystal state (PDB 3SPU) to evaluate **substrate-bound holo complexes and sub-angstrom structures**.

Using the downloaded high-resolution crystal structures (`4HL2`, `4RL0`, `5ZGY`, `5YD7`), Step 11 calculated parallel Elastic Network Models (GNM/ANM), Perturbation Response Scanning (PRS), and directional $\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}$ matrices under **strict parallel cache isolation** and **zero neural network retraining**:

1. **Strict Canonical Isolation Invariant Preserved:**
   * `data/processed/dynamics_3spu.npz` remains **permanently locked and unmodified**.
   * Comparative dynamics are stored in isolated parallel files: `dynamics_4hl2.npz` (1.32 MB), `dynamics_4rl0.npz` (1.32 MB), `dynamics_5zgy.npz` (1.32 MB), and `dynamics_5yd7.npz` (1.35 MB).
   * All trained models, disjoint splits, and test certificates remain 100% frozen and valid.
2. **Sub-Angstrom Backbone Structural Fidelity:**
   Pairwise $\text{C}\alpha$ superposition confirms high conformational conservation between the unliganded Apo state and substrate-bound states:
   * **`4HL2` (Ampicillin-Bound):** $\text{RMSD} = \mathbf{0.696\text{ \AA}}$
   * **`4RL0` (Meropenem-Bound):** $\text{RMSD} = \mathbf{0.710\text{ \AA}}$
   * **`5ZGY` (0.95 Å Resolution at pH 7.5):** $\text{RMSD} = \mathbf{0.957\text{ \AA}}$
3. **M154 Allosteric Driver Invariant Universally Conserved:**
   Across all four NDM-1 crystal structures (`3SPU`, `4HL2`, `4RL0`, `5ZGY`), **M154 exhibits strictly positive driver coupling ($\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}} > 0$) to all 6 catalytic zinc coordinating residues in 100% of evaluations (24 out of 24 measurements)**.
4. **Enhanced Active-Site Coupling in the Meropenem-Bound State (`4RL0`):**
   When Meropenem binds in `4RL0`, M154's directional driver coupling to the catalytic pocket **increases across the board**:
   * $\text{His120}$: $+0.4316 \to \mathbf{+0.4420}$
   * $\text{His122}$: $+0.1565 \to \mathbf{+0.2699}$ ($+72\%$ increase)
   * $\text{Asp124}$: $+0.3189 \to \mathbf{+0.4510}$ ($+41\%$ increase)
   * $\text{His189}$: $+0.2025 \to \mathbf{+0.2437}$ ($+20\%$ increase)
   * $\text{Cys208}$: $+0.2637 \to \mathbf{+0.2734}$ ($+4\%$ increase)
5. **Substrate-Induced Active-Site Loop $L8$ Rigidification:**
   Substrate binding in `4HL2` and `4RL0` induces closure and rigidification of catalytic loop $L8$ (residues 211–226), dropping theoretical fluctuation MSF from $0.2171$ in Apo to $0.2001$ in Ampicillin-bound and $0.1977$ in Meropenem-bound states.
6. **Automated Test Suite Integration:**
