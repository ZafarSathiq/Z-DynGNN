## 3. Law of Total Biological Variance & Experimental Noise Ceilings

To establish the physical upper limit of machine learning performance under irreducible wet-lab experimental noise, the total biological variance was decomposed according to the Law of Total Variance (Hsu et al. 2025 *PNAS*):
$$\text{Var}(y) = \text{Var}(f_{\text{biological}}) + \mathbb{E}[\sigma_\epsilon^2]$$
where $\mathbb{E}[\sigma_\epsilon^2] = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N s_{i, \text{repl}}^2$ is the mean experimental replicate variance across Illumina deep sequencing biological replicates.

The theoretical maximum achievable determination ($R^2_{\max}$), maximum Pearson correlation ($r_{\max}$), and Signal-to-Noise Ratio ($\text{SNR}$) were evaluated across all 9 conditions:
$$R^2_{\max} = 1 - \frac{\mathbb{E}[\sigma_\epsilon^2]}{\text{Var}(y)}, \quad r_{\max} = \sqrt{R^2_{\max}}, \quad \text{SNR} = \frac{\text{Var}(y) - \mathbb{E}[\sigma_\epsilon^2]}{\mathbb{E}[\sigma_\epsilon^2]}$$
