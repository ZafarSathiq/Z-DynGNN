## 1. Biophysical & Structural Dynamics Formulations

### 1.1 Contact Adjacency Indicator Matrix ($A$)
* **Equation Name:** Residue Contact Adjacency Indicator Matrix
* **Formula:**
  $$A_{ij} = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if } i \ne j \quad \text{and} \quad \|\mathbf{r}_i - \mathbf{r}_j\|_2 \le d_0 \\ 0 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
* **Variables:**
  * $\mathbf{r}_i, \mathbf{r}_j \in \mathbb{R}^3$: Cartesian coordinates of the $\text{C}\alpha$ atom of residue $i$ and residue $j$, extracted from PDB 3SPU (1.30 Å resolution).
  * $d_0$: Spatial interaction cutoff distance ($d_0 = 8.0\text{ \AA}$ standard for protein contact networks).
  * $A \in \{0, 1\}^{N \times N}$: Symmetric, hollow ($A_{ii} = 0$) binary matrix, where $N = 228$ resolved residues (residue indices 43 to 270 of NDM-1).
* **Biophysical Meaning:** Defines the physical backbone interaction topology of the folded enzyme. Residues within 8.0 Å share non-covalent contacts (hydrogen bonds, van der Waals, salt bridges).

---

### 1.2 Gaussian Network Model (GNM) Kirchhoff Matrix ($\Gamma$)
* **Equation Name:** GNM Kirchhoff (Graph Laplacian) Connectivity Matrix
* **Formula:**
  $$\Gamma_{ij} = \begin{cases} \sum_{k \ne i} A_{ik} = d_i & \text{if } i = j \\ -A_{ij} & \text{if } i \ne j \end{cases}$$
  $$\Gamma = \mathbf{D} - A$$
* **Variables:**
  * $\Gamma \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times N}$: Kirchhoff matrix.
  * $\mathbf{D} = \text{diag}(d_1, d_2, \dots, d_N)$: Diagonal degree matrix where $d_i$ is the contact coordination number of residue $i$.
* **Biophysical Meaning:** Models the protein as an elastic network of isotropic harmonic springs connecting neighboring residues. The diagonal represents total local structural packing degree.

---

### 1.3 GNM Spectral Decomposition & Fiedler Eigenvalue ($\lambda_2$)
* **Equation Name:** Kirchhoff Eigendecomposition and Algebraic Connectivity
* **Formula:**
  $$\Gamma = \mathbf{V} \mathbf{\Lambda} \mathbf{V}^T = \sum_{k=1}^N \lambda_k \mathbf{v}_k \mathbf{v}_k^T$$
  $$0 = \lambda_1 < \lambda_2 \le \lambda_3 \le \dots \le \lambda_N$$
* **Variables:**
  * $\lambda_k \in \mathbb{R}$: Eigenvalues in ascending order.
  * $\mathbf{v}_k \in \mathbb{R}^N$: Orthogonal eigenvector corresponding to mode $k$.
  * $\lambda_1 = 0$: Rigid-body translational zero mode ($\mathbf{v}_1 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}[1, \dots, 1]^T$).
  * $\lambda_2$: The **Fiedler Eigenvalue** ($\lambda_2 = 0.2832$ for NDM-1).
* **Biophysical Meaning:** $\lambda_2$ quantifies the overall global flexibility and structural stability of the protein fold. Lower $\lambda_2$ indicates flexible hinge regions; $\mathbf{v}_2$ (the Fiedler vector) partitions the enzyme into domain-level motion compartments.

---

### 1.4 GNM Moore–Penrose Pseudo-Inverse Covariance Matrix ($\Gamma^\dagger$)
* **Equation Name:** GNM Fluctuation Covariance Matrix
* **Formula:**
  $$\Gamma^\dagger = \sum_{k=2}^N \frac{1}{\lambda_k} \mathbf{v}_k \mathbf{v}_k^T$$
* **Variables:**
  * $\Gamma^\dagger \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times N}$: Pseudo-inverse matrix omitting the trivial translational zero mode ($k=1$).
* **Biophysical Meaning:** The elements $\Gamma^\dagger_{ij}$ quantify the thermodynamic cross-correlation of equilibrium fluctuations between residue $i$ and residue $j$ under thermal excitation: $\langle \Delta \mathbf{r}_i \cdot \Delta \mathbf{r}_j \rangle = \frac{3 k_B T}{\gamma} \Gamma^\dagger_{ij}$.

---

### 1.5 Theoretical Mean-Square Fluctuation (MSF) Equation
* **Equation Name:** Residue-Specific Theoretical Mean-Square Fluctuation
* **Formula:**
  $$\langle (\Delta r_i)^2 \rangle = \frac{3 k_B T}{\gamma} \Gamma^\dagger_{ii}$$
* **Variables:**
  * $\langle (\Delta r_i)^2 \rangle$: Theoretical equilibrium positional fluctuation variance of residue $i$.
  * $k_B$: Boltzmann constant ($1.3806 \times 10^{-23}\text{ J/K}$).
  * $T$: Absolute temperature ($298.15\text{ K}$).
  * $\gamma$: Effective uniform spring constant ($\gamma = 1.0\text{ kcal}/(\text{mol}\cdot\text{\AA}^2)$).
* **Biophysical Meaning:** Predicts intrinsic flexibility directly from crystal contact topology. High $\Gamma^\dagger_{ii}$ indicates flexible solvent-exposed loops (e.g. active-site loops $L3$ and $L8$); low $\Gamma^\dagger_{ii}$ identifies the rigid hydrophobic core.

---

### 1.6 Crystallographic B-Factor Theoretical Relation
* **Equation Name:** Debye–Waller Isotropic B-Factor Relation
* **Formula:**
  $$B_i^{\text{theor}} = \frac{8 \pi^2}{3} \langle (\Delta r_i)^2 \rangle = \frac{8 \pi^2 k_B T}{\gamma} \Gamma^\dagger_{ii}$$
* **Variables:**
  * $B_i$: Crystallographic thermal B-factor (temperature factor in $\text{\AA}^2$) from X-ray diffraction.
* **Validation Invariant:** In Z-DynGNN, the correlation between theoretical MSF $\Gamma^\dagger_{ii}$ and experimental PDB 3SPU B-factors is **Pearson $r = 0.7077$**, confirming that the elastic network accurately captures physical crystal dynamics.

### 1.7 Anisotropic Network Model (ANM) Hessian Matrix ($H$)
* **Equation Name:** ANM $3N \times 3N$ Directional Hessian Matrix
* **Formula:**
  $$H_{ij}^{3 \times 3} = -\frac{\gamma_{ij}}{\|\mathbf{r}_i - \mathbf{r}_j\|^2} (\mathbf{r}_i - \mathbf{r}_j) (\mathbf{r}_i - \mathbf{r}_j)^T \quad (i \ne j, \text{ edge exists})$$
  $$H_{ii}^{3 \times 3} = -\sum_{j \ne i} H_{ij}^{3 \times 3}$$
* **Variables:**
  * $H \in \mathbb{R}^{3N \times 3N}$: Block Hessian matrix ($3 \times 3$ submatrices per residue pair).
  * $(\mathbf{r}_i - \mathbf{r}_j)$: $3 \times 1$ displacement vector between $\text{C}\alpha$ coordinates.
  * $\gamma_{ij}$: Spring force constant ($\gamma = 1.0$).
* **Biophysical Meaning:** Unlike the scalar GNM, ANM accounts for directional 3D geometric anisotropy, resolving anisotropic deformations along spatial $x, y, z$ axes.

---

### 1.8 ANM Moore–Penrose Pseudo-Inverse ($H^\dagger$)
* **Equation Name:** ANM Directional Covariance Matrix
* **Formula:**
  $$H^\dagger = \sum_{k=7}^{3N} \frac{1}{\lambda_k} \mathbf{u}_k \mathbf{u}_k^T$$
* **Variables:**
  * $H^\dagger \in \mathbb{R}^{3N \times 3N}$: Pseudo-inverse matrix.
  * $k = 1, \dots, 6$: The 6 rigid-body zero modes (3 translations $+ 3$ rotations), where $\lambda_k = 0$.
  * $\mathbf{u}_k \in \mathbb{R}^{3N}$: 3D vibrational normal modes.
* **Biophysical Meaning:** Inverts the physical stiffness tensor to calculate directional dynamic compliance throughout the protein structure.

---

### 1.9 Perturbation Response Scanning (PRS) Displacement Response Matrix ($S$)
* **Equation Name:** PRS Perturbation Response Amplitude Matrix
* **Formula:**
  $$S_{ij} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{3} \text{Tr}\left( H_{ij}^\dagger (H_{ij}^\dagger)^T \right)}$$
* **Variables:**
  * $H_{ij}^\dagger \in \mathbb{R}^{3 \times 3}$: The $3 \times 3$ block of $H^\dagger$ corresponding to residues $i$ and $j$.
  * $S_{ij} \in \mathbb{R}^+$: Root-mean-square displacement amplitude experienced by residue $i$ when an isotropic unit force perturbation $\mathbf{F}_j$ ($\|\mathbf{F}_j\| = 1$) is applied to residue $j$.
* **Biophysical Meaning:** Simulates linear response displacement propagation following mutational or ligand-binding conformational shocks.

---

### 1.10 Dynamic Coupling Index ($\text{DCI}$)
* **Equation Name:** Row-Normalized Dynamic Coupling Index
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{DCI}_{ij} = \frac{S_{ij}}{\frac{1}{N} \sum_{k=1}^N S_{ik}}$$
* **Variables:**
  * $\text{DCI}_{ij}$: Strength of conformational response of residue $i$ when residue $j$ is perturbed, normalized against the average response of residue $i$ to all perturbations across the entire enzyme.
* **Biophysical Meaning:** $\text{DCI}_{ij} > 1.0$ indicates that residue $i$ is coupled more strongly to residue $j$ than to the average protein residue.

---

### 1.11 Asymmetric Dynamic Coupling Index ($\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}$)
* **Equation Name:** Directional Dynamic Coupling Asymmetry
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}(i, j) = \text{DCI}_{ij} - \text{DCI}_{ji}$$
* **Mathematical Properties:**
  1. **Strict Anti-symmetry:** $\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}(i, j) = -\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}(j, i) \implies \text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}^T = -\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}$.
  2. **Hollow Diagonal:** $\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}(i, i) = 0$.
* **Biophysical Interpretation:**
  * If $\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}(i, j) > 0$: Residue $j$ is an **Allosteric Driver** exerting dominant conformational control over residue $i$.
  * If $\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}(i, j) < 0$: Residue $i$ is a **Dynamic Sensor** passively absorbing mechanical perturbations from residue $j$.
* **M154L Clinical Finding:** Residue 154 exhibits positive driver coupling to all 6 catalytic zinc coordinating residues:
  * $\text{His120}$ ($\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}} = +0.4316$)
  * $\text{His122}$ ($\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}} = +0.1565$)
  * $\text{Asp124}$ ($\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}} = +0.3189$)
  * $\text{His189}$ ($\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}} = +0.2025$)
  * $\text{Cys208}$ ($\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}} = +0.2637$)
  * $\text{His250}$ ($\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}} = +0.1174$)

### 1.12 Local Graph Clustering Coefficient ($C_i$)
* **Equation Name:** Watts–Strogatz Local Clustering Coefficient
* **Formula:**
  $$C_i = \frac{2 e_i}{d_i (d_i - 1)} = \frac{\sum_{j, k} A_{ij} A_{jk} A_{ki}}{d_i (d_i - 1)}$$
* **Variables:**
  * $e_i$: Number of actual edges between the neighbors of residue $i$.
  * $d_i$: Contact coordination number of residue $i$.
* **Biophysical Meaning:** Measures local packing density and structural triangulation around residue $i$ ($\mu = 0.5451$ for NDM-1).

---

### 1.13 Normalized Symmetric Graph Laplacian ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{sym}}$)
* **Equation Name:** Normalized Graph Laplacian
* **Formula:**
  $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{sym}} = \mathbf{D}^{-1/2} \Gamma \mathbf{D}^{-1/2} = \mathbf{I} - \mathbf{D}^{-1/2} A \mathbf{D}^{-1/2}$$
* **Mathematical Property:** Eigenvalues satisfy $0 = \mu_1 \le \mu_2 \le \dots \le \mu_N \le 2$. Used to construct smooth transductive spectral embeddings.

## 2. Geometric Deep Learning & Neural Network Equations

### 2.1 Gaussian Radial Basis Function (RBF) Edge Distance Expansion
* **Equation Name:** Continuous Radial Basis Function Edge Encoding
* **Formula:**
  $$\phi_k(d_{ij}) = \exp\left( -\gamma (d_{ij} - \mu_k)^2 \right) \quad \text{for } k = 1, \dots, K$$
  $$\gamma = \frac{0.5}{(\Delta \mu)^2}, \quad \Delta \mu = \frac{d_{\max} - d_{\min}}{K - 1}$$
* **Variables:**
  * $d_{ij} = \|\mathbf{r}_i - \mathbf{r}_j\|_2$: Continuous Euclidean distance in Angstroms between residues $i$ and $j$.
  * $K = 16$: Number of radial centers.
  * $\mu_k \in [0.0\text{ \AA}, 8.0\text{ \AA}]$: Linearly spaced center positions.
  * $\phi(d_{ij}) \in \mathbb{R}^{16}$: Continuous, smooth edge embedding vector.
* **Engineering Purpose:** Prevents discontinuity artifacts caused by hard distance threshold cutoffs.

---

### 2.2 Directional Dynamics Message Passing Aggregation
* **Equation Name:** DCI-Modulated Message Formulation
* **Formula:**
  $$\mathbf{m}_{j \to i} = \text{MLP}_{\text{msg}}\left( \left[ \mathbf{h}_j^{(t)}, \; \mathbf{e}_{ji}, \; \text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}(j, i) \right] \right)$$
  $$\mathbf{m}_i = \sum_{j \in \mathcal{N}(i)} \mathbf{m}_{j \to i}$$
* **Variables:**
  * $\mathbf{h}_j^{(t)} \in \mathbb{R}^{64}$: Hidden state of source node $j$ at message passing layer $t$.
  * $\mathbf{e}_{ji} = \text{Linear}(\phi(d_{ji})) \in \mathbb{R}^{32}$: Edge feature vector.
  * $\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}(j, i) \in \mathbb{R}$: Directional perturbation coupling from source $j$ to target $i$.
  * $\mathcal{N}(i) = \{j : A_{ij} = 1\}$: Set of contact neighbors of residue $i$.
* **Engineering Purpose:** Forces the GNN to condition its latent representations on whether an interacting neighbor is an allosteric driver or a receptive sensor.

---

### 2.3 Residual Node Update & Layer Normalization
* **Equation Name:** Residual Graph Update
* **Formula:**
  $$\mathbf{h}_i^{(t+1)} = \text{LayerNorm}\left( \mathbf{h}_i^{(t)} + \text{MLP}_{\text{update}}\left( \left[ \mathbf{h}_i^{(t)}, \; \mathbf{m}_i \right] \right) \right)$$
* **Variables:**
  * $\text{LayerNorm}(\mathbf{x}) = \frac{\mathbf{x} - \mathbb{E}[\mathbf{x}]}{\sqrt{\text{Var}(\mathbf{x}) + \epsilon}} \odot \mathbf{\gamma} + \mathbf{\beta}$.
* **Engineering Purpose:** Residual connections prevent vanishing gradients across deep message passing layers ($T = 3$).

---

### 2.4 Mutation-Level Representation Concatenation
* **Equation Name:** Mutation Latent State Formulation
* **Formula:**
  $$\mathbf{z}_{\text{mut}} = \left[ \mathbf{h}_p^{(T)}, \; \text{OneHot}(a_{\text{wt}}), \; \text{OneHot}(a_{\text{mut}}) \right] \in \mathbb{R}^{64 + 20 + 20} = \mathbb{R}^{104}$$
* **Variables:**
  * $\mathbf{h}_p^{(T)} \in \mathbb{R}^{64}$: Final message-passing representation at mutated position $p$.
  * $\text{OneHot}(a_{\text{wt}}) \in \{0, 1\}^{20}$: Canonical 20-amino-acid vector of wild-type residue.
  * $\text{OneHot}(a_{\text{mut}}) \in \{0, 1\}^{20}$: Canonical 20-amino-acid vector of mutant residue.
* **Engineering Purpose:** Pairs the structural microenvironment of position $p$ with the chemical substitution transition $a_{\text{wt}} \to a_{\text{mut}}$.

---

### 2.5 Dual-Head Heteroscedastic Outputs ($\hat{\mu}, \hat{\sigma}$)
* **Equation Name:** Predictive Mean and Aleatoric Scale Heads
* **Formula:**
  $$\hat{\mu}(x_i) = \text{MLP}_{\mu}(\mathbf{z}_{\text{mut}}) \in \mathbb{R}$$
  $$\mathbf{s} = \text{clamp}\left( \text{MLP}_{\sigma}(\mathbf{z}_{\text{mut}}), \; s_{\min} = -10.0, \; s_{\max} = 5.0 \right)$$
  $$\hat{\sigma}(x_i) = \exp(0.5 \cdot \mathbf{s}) = \sqrt{\exp(\mathbf{s})}$$
* **Variables:**
  * $\hat{\mu}(x_i)$: Predicted mean fitness effect (log2-fold change).
  * $\mathbf{s} = \log \hat{\sigma}^2$: Log-variance output of the scale network.
  * $\hat{\sigma}(x_i) \in \mathbb{R}^+$: Predicted aleatoric standard deviation.
* **Engineering Purpose:** Parameterizes the instance-specific uncertainty scale to studentize non-conformity residuals.

---

### 2.6 Gaussian Negative Log-Likelihood (NLL) Loss
* **Equation Name:** Heteroscedastic Gaussian NLL Loss Function
* **Formula:**
  $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{NLL}}(y, \hat{\mu}, \hat{\sigma}) = \frac{1}{2} \log(2\pi \hat{\sigma}^2) + \frac{(y - \hat{\mu})^2}{2\hat{\sigma}^2}$$
  $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{batch}} = \frac{1}{B} \sum_{i=1}^B \left[ \frac{1}{2} s_i + \frac{(y_i - \hat{\mu}_i)^2}{2 \exp(s_i)} \right] + \text{const}$$
* **Variables:**
  * $y \in \mathbb{R}$: Ground-truth experimental fitness score from Chen et al. 2024 DMS.
  * $\hat{\mu}$: Predicted mean.
  * $\hat{\sigma}$: Predicted aleatoric scale.
* **Loss Mechanics:**
  1. The quadratic residual term $\frac{(y - \hat{\mu})^2}{2\hat{\sigma}^2}$ forces $\hat{\mu}$ toward $y$.
  2. If the model makes a large error, it can lower the loss by increasing $\hat{\sigma}$.
  3. The penalty $\frac{1}{2} \log \hat{\sigma}^2$ prevents $\hat{\sigma}$ from expanding to infinity.

## 3. Distribution-Free Conformal Prediction & Risk Control Equations

### 3.1 Two-Sided Studentized Non-Conformity Score ($R_i$)
* **Equation Name:** Studentized Absolute Residual Non-Conformity Score
* **Formula:**
  $$R_i = \frac{|y_i - \hat{\mu}(x_i)|}{\max(\hat{\sigma}(x_i), \; \varepsilon)}$$
* **Variables:**
  * $y_i$: Ground-truth fitness value.
  * $\hat{\mu}(x_i)$: Predictive mean from frozen Z-DynGNN.
  * $\hat{\sigma}(x_i)$: Predictive aleatoric scale from frozen Z-DynGNN.
  * $\varepsilon = 10^{-4}$: Numerical stability constant.
* **Statistical Meaning:** Scales the absolute residual by the predicted instance uncertainty. Residues where the model is confident ($\hat{\sigma}$ is small) require smaller residuals to achieve a given score.

---

### 3.2 Finite-Sample Adjusted Quantile Level Formula ($p$)
* **Equation Name:** Angelopoulos–Bates Finite-Sample Conformal Level
* **Formula:**
  $$p = \min\left(1.0, \; \frac{\lceil (m + 1)(1 - \alpha) \rceil}{m}\right)$$
* **Variables:**
  * $m$: Number of exchangeable calibration instances ($m = 32$ position clusters).
  * $\alpha \in (0, 1)$: Target miscoverage budget ($\alpha = 0.05$ for $95.0\%$ confidence).
  * $\lceil \cdot \rceil$: Mathematical ceiling function.
* **Exact Numerical Calculation:**
  For $m = 32$ and $\alpha = 0.05$:
  $$(m + 1)(1 - \alpha) = 33 \times 0.95 = 31.35 \implies \lceil 31.35 \rceil = 32$$
  $$p = \frac{32}{32} = 1.0000$$

---

### 3.3 Conformal Empirical Quantile Function ($\hat{q}$)
* **Equation Name:** Conformal Sample Quantile
* **Formula:**
  $$\hat{q}_{1-\alpha} = \text{Quantile}\left( \{R_1, \dots, R_m\}, \; p \right)$$
* **Mathematical Guarantee:** Under exchangeability of non-conformity scores:
  $$\mathbb{P}\left( R_{m+1} \le \hat{q}_{1-\alpha} \right) \ge 1 - \alpha$$

---

### 3.4 Two-Sided Certified Prediction Interval Bound
* **Equation Name:** Two-Sided Certified Prediction Interval
* **Formula:**
  $$B_i = \hat{q}_{1-\alpha} \cdot \hat{\sigma}(x_i)$$
  $$\mathcal{C}(x_i) = \left[ \hat{\mu}(x_i) - B_i, \; \hat{\mu}(x_i) + B_i \right]$$
* **Mathematical Coverage Theorem:**
  $$\mathbb{P}\left( y_{m+1} \in \mathcal{C}(x_{m+1}) \right) = \mathbb{P}\left( |y_{m+1} - \hat{\mu}(x_{m+1})| \le \hat{q}_{1-\alpha} \hat{\sigma}(x_{m+1}) \right) \ge 1 - \alpha$$
* **Key Property:** Distribution-free validity holds for any sample size $m \ge 1$ without requiring Gaussian or linear residual assumptions.

---

### 3.5 Position-Cluster Aggregated Non-Conformity Score ($R_{\text{pos}}$)
* **Equation Name:** Cluster-Adjusted Non-Conformity Aggregation
* **Formula:**
  $$R_{\text{pos}}(k) = \text{Quantile}\left( \left\{ R_{k, j} : j \in \text{mutations at residue position } k \right\}, \; q_{\text{agg}} \right)$$
* **Variables:**
  * $k \in \{1, \dots, m\}$: Residue position index ($m = 32$ calibration positions).
  * $q_{\text{agg}} = 0.90$: Within-position aggregation percentile.
* **Theoretical Purpose:** Under strict position-disjoint splits, mutations at the same position share local structural dependencies and are not mutually exchangeable. By aggregating scores per position, each **residue position cluster** becomes the exchangeable unit, completely recovering valid finite-sample coverage on unseen test positions.

---

### 3.6 Cluster-Adjusted Conformal Quantile ($\hat{q}_{\text{cluster}}$)
* **Equation Name:** Cluster-Adjusted Conformal Quantile
* **Formula:**
  $$\hat{q}_{\text{cluster}} = \text{Quantile}\left( \{R_{\text{pos}}(1), \dots, R_{\text{pos}}(32)\}, \; \frac{\lceil 33 \times (1 - \alpha) \rceil}{32} \right)$$
* **Production Locked Quantiles ($\alpha = 0.05, q_{\text{agg}} = 0.90$):**

  * Meropenem (`0.25_MEM`): $\hat{q}_{\text{cluster}} = \mathbf{2.210}$ (Test Coverage: **95.10%**)

### 3.7 Signed One-Sided Upper Non-Conformity Score ($R_i^{\text{upper}}$)
* **Equation Name:** Signed Upper Non-Conformity Score
* **Formula:**
  $$R_i^{\text{upper}} = \frac{y_i - \hat{\mu}(x_i)}{\max(\hat{\sigma}(x_i), \; \varepsilon)}$$
* **Statistical Meaning:**
  * $R_i^{\text{upper}} > 0$ when true fitness exceeds the predicted mean ($y_i > \hat{\mu}$).
  * $R_i^{\text{upper}} \le 0$ when true fitness is less than or equal to the predicted mean ($y_i \le \hat{\mu}$).
* **Clinical Purpose:** Focuses strictly on **treatment failure risk (resistance overshoot)**, relaxing the lower bound on inactive variants to contract certified error margins by $\approx 60\%$.

---

### 3.8 One-Sided Upper Conformal Certificate Bound
* **Equation Name:** One-Sided Upper Conformal Prediction Bound
* **Formula:**
  $$B_i^{\text{upper}} = \hat{q}_{\text{up}} \cdot \hat{\sigma}(x_i)$$
  $$\mathcal{C}_{\text{upper}}(x_i) = \left( -\infty, \; \hat{\mu}(x_i) + B_i^{\text{upper}} \right]$$
  $$U_{\text{upper}}(x_i) = \hat{\mu}(x_i) + \hat{q}_{\text{up}} \cdot \hat{\sigma}(x_i)$$
* **Mathematical Guarantee:**
  $$\mathbb{P}\left( y_i \le U_{\text{upper}}(x_i) \right) \ge 1 - \alpha$$
* **Production Upper Quantiles ($\alpha = 0.05$):**
  * Ampicillin: $\hat{q}_{\text{up}} = \mathbf{2.161}$ (Upper Coverage: **99.67%**, Mean Margin: **4.71 log2-units**)
  * Cefotaxime: $\hat{q}_{\text{up}} = \mathbf{1.642}$ (Upper Coverage: **99.02%**, Mean Margin: **3.29 log2-units**)
  * Meropenem: $\hat{q}_{\text{up}} = \mathbf{1.844}$ (Upper Coverage: **100.0%**, Mean Margin: **3.71 log2-units**)

### 3.9 Signed One-Sided Lower Non-Conformity Score ($R_i^{\text{lower}}$)
* **Equation Name:** Signed Lower Non-Conformity Score
* **Formula:**
  $$R_i^{\text{lower}} = \frac{\hat{\mu}(x_i) - y_i}{\max(\hat{\sigma}(x_i), \; \varepsilon)}$$
* **One-Sided Lower Certificate Bound:**
  $$L_{\text{lower}}(x_i) = \hat{\mu}(x_i) - \hat{q}_{\text{lower}} \cdot \hat{\sigma}(x_i)$$
  $$\mathcal{C}_{\text{lower}}(x_i) = \left[ L_{\text{lower}}(x_i), \; +\infty \right)$$
  $$\mathbb{P}\left( y_i \ge L_{\text{lower}}(x_i) \right) \ge 1 - \alpha$$

### 3.10 Winkler Interval Score (Proper Scoring Rule)
* **Equation Name:** Mean Interval Score (Winkler Score)
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{Score}_{\alpha}(L_i, U_i, y_i) = (U_i - L_i) + \frac{2}{\alpha} (L_i - y_i) \mathbb{I}(y_i < L_i) + \frac{2}{\alpha} (y_i - U_i) \mathbb{I}(y_i > U_i)$$
* **Variables:**
  * $(U_i - L_i) = 2 B_i$: Interval width.
  * $\mathbb{I}(\cdot)$: Indicator function equal to 1 if condition holds, 0 otherwise.
  * $\frac{2}{\alpha}$: Linear penalty multiplier for boundary violations ($\frac{2}{0.05} = 40.0$).
* **Statistical Meaning:** Proper scoring rule that simultaneously penalizes wider intervals and out-of-bounds miscoverage errors. Lower score indicates superior efficiency.

---

### 3.11 Empirical Coverage Verification Formula
* **Equation Name:** Empirical Sample Coverage
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{Cov}_{\text{emp}} = \frac{1}{N_{\text{test}}} \sum_{i=1}^{N_{\text{test}}} \mathbb{I}\left( L_i \le y_i \le U_i \right)$$
* **Target Acceptance Invariant:**
  $$\text{Cov}_{\text{emp}} \ge 1 - \alpha - \epsilon$$

## 4. Experimental Noise Ceiling & Assay Statistics

### 4.1 Total Assay Variance Decomposition
* **Equation Name:** Law of Total Variance for Biological Assays
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{Var}(y) = \text{Var}(f_{\text{biological}}) + \mathbb{E}[\sigma_\epsilon^2]$$
* **Variables:**
  * $\text{Var}(y)$: Total empirical variance observed across all mutations in condition.
  * $\text{Var}(f_{\text{biological}})$: Explainable variance attributable to true biological fitness.
  * $\mathbb{E}[\sigma_\epsilon^2] = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^N s_{i, \text{repl}}^2$: Mean experimental replicate variance across biological replicates in Chen et al. 2024.

---

### 4.2 Theoretical Noise Ceilings ($R^2_{\max}$ and $r_{\max}$)
* **Equation Name:** Maximum Achievable Correlation Under Replicate Noise (Hsu et al. 2025 PNAS)
* **Formula:**
  $$R^2_{\max} = 1 - \frac{\mathbb{E}[\sigma_\epsilon^2]}{\text{Var}(y)}$$
  $$r_{\max} = \sqrt{R^2_{\max}} = \sqrt{1 - \frac{\mathbb{E}[\sigma_\epsilon^2]}{\text{Var}(y)}}$$
* **Empirical Values Calculated on Chen et al. 2024 (Ampicillin 256 µg/mL):**
  * $\text{Var}(y) = 9.7533$
  * $\mathbb{E}[\sigma_\epsilon^2] = 0.2642$
  * $\text{SNR} = \frac{\text{Var}(y) - \mathbb{E}[\sigma_\epsilon^2]}{\mathbb{E}[\sigma_\epsilon^2]} = \mathbf{35.97}$
  * $R^2_{\max} = 1 - \frac{0.2642}{9.7533} = \mathbf{0.9729}$
  * $r_{\max} = \sqrt{0.9729} = \mathbf{0.9864}$

---

### 4.3 Normalized Performance Metrics
* **Equation Name:** Noise-Ceiling Normalized Correlation and Determination
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{Norm } r = \frac{r_{\text{test}}}{r_{\max}}, \quad \text{Norm } R^2 = \frac{R^2_{\text{test}}}{R^2_{\max}}$$
* **Z-DynGNN Performance on Held-Out Test Positions:**
  * Raw Pearson $r = 0.6339 \implies \text{Norm } r = \frac{0.6339}{0.9864} = \mathbf{0.6426}$ (**64.3% of physical ceiling**).
  * Raw $R^2 = 0.3161 \implies \text{Norm } R^2 = \frac{0.3161}{0.9729} = \mathbf{0.3249}$.

## 5. Clinical Decision-Theoretic Classification Formulations

### 5.1 Two-Sided Ternary Decision Rule
* **Equation Name:** Symmetric Certified Resistance Breakpoint Classification
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{Decision}(L_i, U_i) = \begin{cases} \text{Certified Resistant} & \text{if } L_i \ge \tau_{\text{res}} \\ \text{Certified Susceptible} & \text{if } U_i < \tau_{\text{susc}} \\ \text{Ambiguous (Triage for Lab)} & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
* **Standard Clinical Thresholds:**
  * $\tau_{\text{res}} = -2.0$ log2-units (retains carbapenemase resistance).
  * $\tau_{\text{susc}} = -5.0$ log2-units (catalytic loss-of-function; drug effective).

---

### 5.2 One-Sided Upper Safety Screening Rule
* **Equation Name:** Certified Treatment Failure Prevention Screening
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{Call}_{\text{upper}}(U_{\text{upper}}) = \begin{cases} \text{Certified Safe / Non-Resistant} & \text{if } U_{\text{upper}}(x_i) < \tau_{\text{res}} \\ \text{Potential Resistance Risk} & \text{if } U_{\text{upper}}(x_i) \ge \tau_{\text{res}} \end{cases}$$
* **Guarantee:** With $\ge 95\%$ confidence, true fitness $y_i$ cannot exceed $U_{\text{upper}}$. If $U_{\text{upper}} < -2.0$, treatment failure is certified not to occur.

---

### 5.3 False Safety Critical Error Rate
* **Equation Name:** Type II Clinical Patient Safety Error Rate
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{False Safety Rate} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N_{\text{test}}} \mathbb{I}(y_i \ge \tau_{\text{res}} \quad \text{and} \quad \text{Call}_i = \text{Certified Safe})}{\sum_{i=1}^{N_{\text{test}}} \mathbb{I}(y_i \ge \tau_{\text{res}})}$$
* **Clinical Invariant:** In Z-DynGNN, this error rate is strictly **0 out of 729 test evaluations (0.00%)**.

## 6. Comprehensive Alphabetical Glossary of Acronyms & Terms

### **A**
* **AA (Amino Acid):** One of the 20 canonical chemical building blocks of proteins.
* **ANISOU:** PDB file record storing the anisotropic displacement parameters (6 tensor coefficients per atom).
* **ANM (Anisotropic Network Model):** Biophysical 3D elastic network model that accounts for directional anisotropy by defining a $3N \times 3N$ Cartesian Hessian matrix.
* **Anti-symmetry:** Mathematical property of a square matrix where $M^T = -M$. Exhibited by $\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}$.

### **B**
* **B-Factor (Debye–Waller Temperature Factor):** Crystallographic metric indicating the thermal mobility and displacement variance of atoms in X-ray structures ($\text{\AA}^2$).
* **BLDB (Beta-Lactamase Database):** Curated public biological repository cataloging all known clinical and natural $\beta$-lactamase protein variants recovered from hospital patients worldwide.

### **C**
* **C$\alpha$ (Alpha Carbon):** Central structural carbon atom in each amino acid backbone. Contact graphs are constructed by mapping $\text{C}\alpha\text{--}\text{C}\alpha$ Euclidean distances.
* **Carbapenem:** Class of highly potent $\beta$-lactam antibiotics (e.g., Meropenem, Imipenem, Ertapenem) reserved as last-line treatments for severe multidrug-resistant bacterial infections.
* **Cephalosporin:** Broad class of $\beta$-lactam antibiotics; third-generation cephalosporins (e.g., Cefotaxime) are major substrates hydrolyzed by NDM-1.
* **CLSI (Clinical and Laboratory Standards Institute):** Global organization setting international clinical breakpoints and MIC interpretation standards.
* **Conformal Prediction:** Statistical framework developed by Vladimir Vovk that produces finite-sample, distribution-free prediction sets with rigorous mathematical coverage guarantees.

### **D**
* **DCI (Dynamic Coupling Index):** Normalized metric quantifying the mechanical displacement response of a residue relative to the mean whole-protein response under perturbation.
* **$\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}$ (Asymmetric Dynamic Coupling Index):** Directional dynamic coupling metric ($\text{DCI}_{ij} - \text{DCI}_{ji}$) identifying allosteric drivers ($\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}} > 0$) versus dynamic sensors ($\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}} < 0$).
* **DMS (Deep Mutational Scanning):** High-throughput wet-lab biological assay combining saturation mutagenesis with Illumina deep sequencing to measure the fitness effects of thousands of protein mutations simultaneously.

### **E**
* **EC50 (Half-Maximal Effective Concentration):** Laboratory concentration of an antibiotic at which half of bacterial growth is inhibited.
* **EGNN (Equivariant Graph Neural Network):** Geometric deep learning architecture whose latent coordinate and feature representations are invariant or equivariant to 3D translations, rotations, and reflections.
* **ENM (Elastic Network Model):** Coarse-grained biophysical model representing protein coordinates as nodes connected by linear harmonic springs.
* **Epistasis:** Non-linear interaction between multiple mutational substitutions where the combined fitness effect differs from the sum of individual effects.
* **ESM (Evolutionary Scale Modeling):** Family of deep transformer protein language models (e.g., ESM-2, ESM-3) trained on hundreds of millions of biological sequences.
* **EUCAST (European Committee on Antimicrobial Susceptibility Testing):** Clinical standards committee determining European susceptibility breakpoints.

### **F**
* **Fiedler Eigenvalue ($\lambda_2$):** Second smallest eigenvalue of the graph Laplacian matrix $\Gamma$. Measures graph algebraic connectivity and resistance to topological partition.

### **G**
* **GELU (Gaussian Error Linear Unit):** Smooth non-linear activation function: $\text{GELU}(x) = x \Phi(x) = x P(X \le x), X \sim \mathcal{N}(0, 1)$.
* **GNM (Gaussian Network Model):** 1D isotropic elastic network model describing structural fluctuations via the $N \times N$ scalar Kirchhoff Laplacian matrix $\Gamma$.
* **GNN (Graph Neural Network):** Deep neural network architecture operating directly on relational graph graphs via message passing.

### **H**
* **Hessian Matrix ($H$):** Second-order partial derivative matrix of physical potential energy with respect to atomic coordinates. In ANM, of shape $3N \times 3N$.
* **Heteroscedasticity:** Statistical property where the variance of prediction residuals varies across different input instances, parameterized in Z-DynGNN by predicting $\hat{\sigma}(x_i)$.

### **K**
* **Kirchhoff Matrix ($\Gamma$):** Graph Laplacian matrix $\Gamma = \mathbf{D} - A$ used in the Gaussian Network Model.
* **Kim–Vu Sandwich Theorem:** Combinatorial probabilistic theorem establishing an asymptotic coupling between random $d$-regular graph ensembles $\mathcal{G}(n, d)$ and binomial Erdős–Rényi graphs $G(n, p)$. Proven inapplicable to deterministic protein contact networks in Correction v1.

### **L**
* **Laplacian ($\mathcal{L}$):** Discrete differential graph operator quantifying diffusion and structural connectivity across networks.
* **Leakage (Data Leakage):** Flaw where features or targets from the test set contaminate model training or calibration. Prevented in Z-DynGNN by strict position-disjoint partitioning.

### **M**
* **M154L:** Canonical clinical allosteric super-variant of NDM-1 (substitution of Methionine with Leucine at position 154) prevalent in NDM-4, NDM-5, and NDM-7. Rigidifies the $\alpha3\text{--}L8\text{--}\beta8$ network to preserve zinc affinity under physiological metal starvation.
* **M248L:** Active-site cavity loop mutation in NDM-22 causing hyper-potent catalytic turnover of Meropenem ($y = +1.22$).
* **MIC (Minimum Inhibitory Concentration):** Lowest concentration of an antimicrobial agent ($\mu\text{g/mL}$) that visibly inhibits the in vitro growth of a bacterial isolate.
* **Moore–Penrose Pseudo-Inverse ($M^\dagger$):** Generalized matrix inverse satisfying $M M^\dagger M = M$. Used to invert singular Laplacian and Hessian matrices by removing translational/rotational zero modes.
* **MSF (Mean-Square Fluctuation):** $\langle (\Delta r_i)^2 \rangle$; the thermal equilibrium positional variance of a residue.

### **N**
* **NDM-1 (New Delhi Metallo-$\beta$-lactamase-1):** B1 subclass zinc-dependent carbapenemase enzyme (270 amino acids) conferring broad resistance to almost all $\beta$-lactam antibiotics.
* **NLL (Negative Log-Likelihood):** Loss function based on minimizing the negative logarithm of the likelihood of observed targets under predicted probability distributions.
* **NPV (Negative Predictive Value):** Clinical classification metric: $\text{NPV} = \frac{\text{TN}}{\text{TN} + \text{FN}}$. Measures the probability that an instance predicted as susceptible is truly susceptible.

### **P**
* **PAC-Bayes (Probably Approximately Correct Bayes):** Theoretical machine learning framework bounding the expected generalization loss $\mathbb{E}[\ell]$ of stochastic neural network weight distributions $Q$.
* **PDB (Protein Data Bank):** Global archive of 3D macromolecular structural data. 3SPU is the 1.30 Å crystal structure of native NDM-1.
* **pLDDT (Predicted Local Distance Difference Test):** Confidence score in $[0, 100]$ output by AlphaFold measuring local structural confidence; heuristic metric that does not provide finite-sample statistical coverage.
* **PLM (Protein Language Model):** Self-supervised transformer trained on biological protein sequences (e.g. ESM-2).
* **Position-Disjoint Split:** Partitioning strategy where entire residue positions are held out: $\mathcal{P}_{\text{train}} \cap \mathcal{P}_{\text{calib}} \cap \mathcal{P}_{\text{test}} = \emptyset$.
* **PPV (Positive Predictive Value / Precision):** $\text{PPV} = \frac{\text{TP}}{\text{TP} + \text{FP}}$.
* **PRS (Perturbation Response Scanning):** Biophysical technique calculating displacement matrices $S_{ij}$ under isotropic force kicks to identify allosteric communication pathways.

### **R**
* **RBF (Radial Basis Function):** Real-valued continuous kernel function whose value depends only on Euclidean distance from a center: $\phi(d) = \exp(-\gamma(d - \mu)^2)$.
* **Residue:** An individual amino acid unit incorporated within a polypeptide protein chain.
* **RMSE (Root Mean Square Error):** Standard deviation of prediction residuals: $\sqrt{\frac{1}{N}\sum (y_i - \hat{\mu}_i)^2}$.

### **S**
* **SiLU (Sigmoid Linear Unit / Swish):** Non-linear activation function: $\text{SiLU}(x) = x \cdot \sigma(x) = \frac{x}{1 + e^{-x}}$.
* **SNR (Signal-to-Noise Ratio):** Ratio of explainable biological variance to experimental replicate error variance. $\text{SNR} = 35.97$ for Ampicillin DMS.
* **Studentized Residual:** Normalized error residual divided by the estimated scale: $R = \frac{|y - \hat{\mu}|}{\hat{\sigma}}$.

### **T**
* **Topology-Conditioned Conformal Prediction:** Stratifying conformal non-conformity quantiles across 2D structural bins $\mathcal{S}(i)$ defined by coordination number $d_i$ and allosteric driver score $\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}$.

### **W**
* **Wild-Type (WT):** The native, unmutated reference biological sequence or crystal structure of an organism or protein.
* **Winkler Score:** Proper scoring rule evaluating prediction intervals by combining width with penalized out-of-bounds boundary violations.

### **Z**
* **Z-DynGNN:** *Z-score Calibrated Dynamics-Aware Graph Neural Network*. The framework pairing biophysical directional dynamics ($\text{DCI}_{\text{asym}}$) with heteroscedastic heads and distribution-free conformal risk control.
* **Zinc Coordinating Residues:** The 6 conserved catalytic active-site residues of NDM-1 that coordinate the two essential catalytic zinc ions: $\text{His120}$ (Ligand 1), $\text{His122}$ (Ligand 2), $\text{Asp124}$ (Ligand 3), $\text{His189}$ (Ligand 4), $\text{Cys208}$ (Ligand 5), and $\text{His250}$ (Ligand 6).
