## 4. Technical Analysis of Persistent Biological Anomalies

### 4.1 M154L Allosteric Point-Estimate Bias
Review v4 specifically noted that the model's predictive mean $\hat{\mu}(\text{M154L})$ remains strongly negative while true fitness is positive:
* **Empirical Profile:**
  * Ampicillin: True $y = +0.485$, Predicted $\hat{\mu} = -5.863$, $\hat{\sigma} = 2.748$
  * Cefotaxime: True $y = +0.032$, Predicted $\hat{\mu} = -3.360$, $\hat{\sigma} = 2.235$
  * Meropenem: True $y = +0.192$, Predicted $\hat{\mu} = -3.167$, $\hat{\sigma} = 2.213$
* **Biophysical Root Cause:**
  Residue 154 is situated on the solvent-exposed $\alpha3$ helix. In standard globular proteins, mutating solvent-exposed residues with high flexibility typically has neutral or destabilizing fitness effects. Standard graph convolution aggregates these local features, leading the network to predict a deleterious drop ($\\hat{\\mu} < -3$).
  However, NDM-1 M154L operates through a specialized allosteric mechanism demonstrated by Huang et al. 2025 (*JACS*): it rigidifies the $\alpha3\text{--}L8\text{--}\beta8$ loop network, increasing affinity for the second catalytic zinc ion and preventing metal starvation under physiological immune defenses.
* **Conformal Coverage Nuance:**
  Under the two-sided interval, M154L is contained across all three drugs because the wide interval ($B \approx 4.8\text{--}9.8$) absorbs the point bias. Under the one-sided upper bound, M154L is contained on CTX ($+0.032 \le +0.315$) and MEM ($+0.192 \le +0.888$), but exceeds the upper bound on AMP ($+0.485 > +0.074$). This confirms Review v4's assessment that containment on AMP under two-sided bounds was driven by interval width rather than point accuracy.
