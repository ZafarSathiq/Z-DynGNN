## 5. In-Depth Biophysical Analysis of Epistasis Mechanisms

### 5.1 Synergistic Buffering (`epi3 K211Y + Y229W`, $\epsilon = +1.87$)
* **The Phenotype:**
  * Singly, `K211Y` is a catastrophic loss-of-function mutation: $\text{EC}_{50}$ plummets from $454.6\,\mu\text{g/mL}$ to **$2.0\,\mu\text{g/mL}$** ($\log_2 = -7.82$).
  * The simple linear additive model predicts that pairing it with `Y229W` ($-1.08$) would cause even deeper collapse: $y_{\text{add}} = -7.82 - 1.08 = \mathbf{-8.90}$.
  * In the wet lab, the actual double mutant exhibits an $\text{EC}_{50}$ of **$3.5\,\mu\text{g/mL}$** ($\log_2 = -7.03$).
  * **The Synergistic Gain:** The double mutant survives **$+1.87$ log2-units higher** than the additive prediction.
* **Biophysical Mechanism:**
  * Lys211 is located on the flexible active-site loop $L8$. Mutating it to bulky Tyrosine disrupts loop orientation.
  * Tyr229 sits across the opposing $\beta$-sheet hairpin. Introducing Tryptophan (`Y229W`) restores compensatory hydrophobic contacts that sterically buffer and stabilize loop $L8$.
* **Conformal Protection:** Z-DynGNN's accumulated uncertainty scale ($\hat{\sigma}_{\text{comb}} = 3.73, B = \pm 13.2$) safely contained this non-linear synergistic rescue within $[-22.8, +3.7]$.

### 5.2 Deleterious Interference (`epi5 A204L + I259L`, $\epsilon = -1.10$)
* **The Phenotype:**
  * Singly: `A204L` ($-4.70$) and `I259L` ($-1.11$) have additive expectation $-5.81$.
  * Actual physical double mutant collapses to **$-6.91$** ($\epsilon = -1.10$).
* **Biophysical Mechanism:**
  * Both Ala204 and Ile259 are buried in the hydrophobic core. Substituting larger hydrophobic side chains simultaneously induces steric packing clashes that accelerate cooperative thermodynamic unfolding.
* **Conformal Protection:** Successfully contained within $[-24.5, +4.1]$.
