## 3. Biophysical Analysis of Epistatic Interactions

### 3.1 Synergistic Buffering (`epi3 K211Y + Y229W`, $\epsilon = +1.87$)
* **Mechanism:** Residue 211 is located in the catalytic loop $L8$. Individually, `K211Y` causes devastating functional collapse ($\text{EC}_{50} = 2.0\,\mu\text{g/mL}$, fitness $-7.82$).
* However, residue 229 resides across the $\beta$-sheet hairpin. Introducing `Y229W` provides steric compensation that partially rescues the active-site pocket geometry ($\text{EC}_{50} = 3.5\,\mu\text{g/mL}$, fitness $-7.03$).
* The actual double mutant performs **$+1.87$ log2-units better than the simple additive sum ($-8.90$)**.

### 3.2 Deleterious Interference (`epi5 A204L + I259L`, $\epsilon = -1.10$)
* **Mechanism:** Individually, `A204L` and `I259L` produce mild/moderate decreases. But combining both hydrophobic packing perturbations in the core causes steric crowding, accelerating cooperative unfolding ($\epsilon = -1.10$).

### 3.3 Decisive Clinical Safety Call on the Triple Mutant
* On the triple mutant `epi5 A204L + I246V + I259L`, the accumulating structural perturbations yield a tight, decisive one-sided upper bound:
  $$U_{\text{upper}} = \mathbf{-5.6\text{ log2-units}} < -2.0 \implies \mathbf{\text{Certified Safe / Susceptible}}$$
* **Clinical Significance:** Z-DynGNN mathematically certifies with $\ge 95\%$ certainty that this triple combination extinguishes carbapenemase resistance, proving that the model can issue decisive safety calls on multi-mutant strains without wet-lab testing.
